Implemented contract:
- IRIS1 encoder start fails with -EOPNOTSUPP if firmware requests
  HFI_BUFFER_INTERNAL_PERSIST and the secure auxiliary device is unavailable.
- A missing secure DT child keeps the proven decoder available; the encoder
  remains blocked by the fail-closed guard.
- SM8150 V2 uses a separate platform child on SID 0x2304.
- The child DMA aperture is [0x01000000, 0x25800000); low IOVA space is
  reserved explicitly.
- Only a consumer declaring qcom,secure-vmid=CP_NON_PIXEL activates the new
  ARM SMMU ownership path.
- Every page-table allocation and SCM ownership extent is PAGE_ALIGN(size).
  This covers a 32-bit-IAS top-level table that can request only 64 bytes.
- Newly allocated page-table pages stay HLOS read/write and are shared
  CP_NON_PIXEL read-only through qcom_scm_assign_mem().
- Freed page-table pages are returned only after IOTLB invalidation. Ownership
  rollback failure poisons the domain and retains the affected pages.
- The page-table free callback copies allocation size while holding its lock;
  it does not dereference the tracking object after a racing sync may free it.
- IRIS1 encoder INTERNAL_PERSIST uses
  DMA_ATTR_FORCE_CONTIGUOUS|DMA_ATTR_NO_KERNEL_MAPPING.
- The driver resolves and verifies every 4 KiB IOVA-to-physical step across
  the allocation before making one contiguous SCM ownership transfer.
- The buffer is zeroed by the DMA allocator, lies inside the secure aperture,
  and is transferred HLOS -> CP_NON_PIXEL read/write before HFI_SET_BUFFERS.
- Teardown performs HFI_UNSET_BUFFERS, returns data ownership to HLOS, then
  removes the DMA mapping and frees memory.
- If HFI unregister or SCM ownership rollback fails, the buffer is retained.
- Scratch and all non-persist buffers keep their existing non-secure DMA path.
- Boot/runtime markers expose secure-page-table READY and persist
  ASSIGN/RECLAIM with VMID, IOVA, and extent.

Scope boundary:
These are source and build facts. No phone has booted this implementation and
no hardware encoder success is claimed.
